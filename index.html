06

BARE-METAL RELIABILITY

Stable disk mounts across node reboots

Challenge
Disk device names such as sda and sdb could swap after a server reboot, putting expected mount paths at risk.
My approach
Created a MachineConfig-based solution that identifies the intended disk using its label and mounts it by that stable identifier instead of relying on the changing device name.
Outcome
Made disk mounting resilient to device-name changes across reboots without relying on the sda or sdb ordering.
  • OpenShift
  • MachineConfig
  • RHCOS
  • Linux storage
